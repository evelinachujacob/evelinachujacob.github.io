Calbre in your palm

Interaction prototypeHand trackingBreath input2026

A 3D prototype of Calbre that you hold and use with your own hand and breath, running in the browser with a webcam and a mic.

Hold up a hand and Calbre sits on it, blower towards your fingertips. Blow, and the screen reads your breath and shows a result. Pull both hands apart to see what is inside.

See-through hologramLooked like a toySolid, in Calbre’s real colour
Ring filled with breath strengthFelt like a game meterReads while you blow, shows a result after
Spread fingers to open itCame apart while holding itPull two hands apart

Inspired by a hand-tracking demo I came across online. Built with MediaPipe and Three.js, prototyped with AI-assisted coding.
