shrimp farming to the state with black tiger shrimp.

01  Spread

The ponds follow the brackish water — a thin strip hugging the coast, nowhere else.

02  Crowding

Farms pack in shoulder to shoulder, drawing from and draining into the same canals.

03  Disease

One sick pond is enough. Disease moves through the shared water, and whole clusters go down together.

Farmers switched to specific pathogen-free vannamei — hardier, faster-growing, and better suited to scale — right as disease was starting to devastate competitors elsewhere in Asia.

Across the globe

In 2024–25 India shipped 7.4 lakh tonnes of frozen shrimp, most of it farmed in Andhra Pradesh. Six markets took nine-tenths of it.

    Source: MPEDA, India's frozen shrimp exports by market, 2024–25. The EU, South-East Asia and the Middle East are MPEDA's own groupings; their countries are lit approximately.
