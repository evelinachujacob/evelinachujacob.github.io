System-level insights

1. Farmer as risk concentration point: the farmer intersects with seed, inputs, water, disease, finance, market and regulatory risks.

2. Disease is systemic: a farm-level disease event can propagate through seed, water, neighbouring farms, harvest and processing.

3. Risks compound: input-cost pressure can encourage intensification, which increases water-quality and disease risk, potentially leading to crop loss and further financial pressure.

4. Information is asymmetric: biological and economic risks can travel through the network faster than reliable information reaches every actor.

5. Export houses act as downstream filters: problems originating at farms can become quality, residue, traceability or compliance issues at processing/export level.

Analytical matrix based on the risk relationships discussed from the Dakshin Foundation Social-Ecological Comprehensive Report on Andhra Pradesh shrimp aquaculture (2025). Ratings are an analytical interpretation for system mapping, not numerical measurements from the report.
