Info

I’m Evelin, a fourth-year Industrial Design student at NID Andhra Pradesh, exploring the intersection of human behaviour, interaction design and intelligent products.

I’m interested in how people interact with products and the experiences they take away.

I believe products work the same way. Beyond what they do, it’s how they make people feel that makes them memorable.
