Design Message

  • Futuristic geometry → cohesive
  • Opposites United → balanced contrasts, emotional tension
  • Tech-forward, premium → modern, elegant, innovative

Interior CMF Analysis


Dark charcoal as the dominant base creates a cockpit like focus

offwhite contrast on the steering wheel rim acts as an accent, evoking sense of approachability

Crosshatching texture in deep charcoal shade across the dashboard

Brushed metallic on console body panel directional grain, satin graphite finish

quietly modern, tech forward, premium ,Visual Continuity, unified surface language,

Interior Seating CMF

  • Interior: Dual-tone SynTex + suede (Saturn Black / Misty Grey)
  • Seating: Vegan leather/suede with heating and ventilation gives family friendly comfort for long drives.
  • The black and off-white contrast evokes a sense of sportiness because high contrast graphic lines are instinctively read by the eye as fast and enhances the sporty look
  • the sweeping vertical lines of the insert panels draw the eye upward, giving the seat a taller, more dynamic silhouette

The rear seats feel geometric and architectural because of the white panel cuts near the headrest, while the front seats feels more sculpted.
